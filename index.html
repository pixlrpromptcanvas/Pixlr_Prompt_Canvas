Learner's Companion

Good prompts are the difference between mediocre AI outputs and high-value, actionable results. Follow this simple 3-step cycle to master prompting:

1

Identify Your Task

What are you trying to accomplish? Pick a category that aligns with your goal:

  • Need to simplify complex topics? → Explain simply
  • Need to choose between options? → Compare
  • Stuck creatively? → List ideas
  • Need brevity? → Make it shorter

2

Customize the Template

Open a prompt accordion, use the interactive form fields to type your own specific details, or copy the template and edit it directly in your AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Designs.AI, Gemini).

3

Review and Refine

Analyze the output. If it needs adjustments, don't restart. Simply prompt: "Close, but make it shorter" or "Add 2 more options" or "Tone it down and make it more professional".

The Prompt Anatomy

Every template in this toolkit follows a proven architectural pattern:

Task + Context + Audience + Purpose + Tone + Format + Constraints

By specifying these details, you direct the AI's persona, knowledge-base, and output format for precise execution.

  • Copy exactly, then customize: Avoid typing templates from memory. Copy them fully to maintain structural integrity, then fill the fields.
  • Be granular in brackets: "Gen Z Instagram users who love organic skincare" yields far superior outcomes compared to just "Instagram users".
  • Combine keywords: You can merge templates. E.g., "Summarise this article" + "Make it shorter" = "Summarise this concept simply in under 100 words".
  • Save working prompts: When you hit a perfect output, save the exact prompt structure you used. Build a customized playbook for your specific brand.
  • Time-to-Output Expectation: Modern LLMs take 10 to 60 seconds to execute these complex instructions. The patience is rewarded with production-ready drafts.

I copied a prompt but the output doesn't feel right. What do I do?

Do not start over. Give feedback to the AI. E.g., "This is close, but rewrite it to be much friendlier and add a relatable example." AI works best through iterative conversation.

Can I combine multiple templates?

Yes. You can copy the structure of "Summarise" and "Turn into a table" and combine them: "Summarise the following meeting transcript and organize the action items into a table."

What do brackets [like this] mean?

The brackets indicate variables. You need to replace them with your own details. On this page, you can type directly into the input fields generated for each accordion to fill them automatically!

Which AI tool works best with these prompts?

These are universal structures. They work perfectly in Designs.AI, ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, and other LLMs.

How do I get more variety?

Use the "Create 5 options" template, or instruct the AI: "Provide three distinct options, one written in a playful marketing tone, one highly professional, and one focused solely on direct benefits."

How do I ensure facts are correct?

AI can sometimes hallucinate details. Implement a simple verification step in your routine: Read → Review → Verify → Use. Always verify critical facts, statistics, or legal items.
